Privacy Policy for RemiMinder

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Contact: privacy@remiminder.ai

Website: https://remiminderai.com/privacy

Overview

RemiMinder helps MyCare users and caregivers manage medications, visit recordings, reminders, and care documents. This policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone (optional), role (MyCare or caregiver), authentication identifiers from Firebase Auth.
  • Care information: medication reminders, visit audio recordings, AI-generated visit summaries, scanned medication labels/documents, care-team sharing preferences.
  • Device data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications only; device language and timezone for scheduling reminders.
  • Consent records: timestamps when you accept microphone, camera, and AI processing consent before recording or scanning.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising IDs or third-party analytics SDKs in the mobile app.

How We Use Information

  • Provide medication reminders and local/push notifications.
  • Record, transcribe, and summarize care visits using Google Cloud Speech-to-Text and Google Gemini (via our secure backend).
  • Scan documents with AI to extract medication and care details.
  • Share selected care information with caregivers you invite and authorize.
  • Operate authentication, sync, and account management.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted processors including Google Firebase (authentication, messaging, Firestore), Google Cloud Platform (storage and APIs), and Google AI services for transcription and summarization. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Permissions (Android / iOS)

Camera

Used to scan medication labels, documents, and other care documents. You must accept in-app consent before scanning.

Microphone

Used only while you actively record a visit. Recording requires explicit in-app consent. We do not record in the background.

Notifications

Used for medication reminders and care alerts. You can disable notifications in device settings or in the app.

Exact alarms (Android)

Used to deliver medication reminders at scheduled times when the app is not open.

We do not request broad device storage, SMS, location, or contacts access for core app features.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Profile or Privacy Settings). This permanently deletes your account and associated data on our servers. To export data or request deletion of specific care records, contact privacy@remiminder.ai.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Authentication tokens are stored in the device secure keystore. Care information is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with access controls.

Important Notice

RemiMinder is a personal caregiving organizer, not a substitute for advice from your care team. AI-generated notes are informational only.

Children

RemiMinder is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
